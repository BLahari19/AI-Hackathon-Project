 🚀 NetworkIQ – AI Inventory Optimization

 📌 Overview
NetworkIQ is an AI-powered inventory optimization system that helps retailers manage inventory across multiple warehouses. The system analyzes demand, inventory levels, warehouse capacity, and transfer costs to recommend the best inventory placement and stock transfers.

The goal is to ensure the **right product is available at the right warehouse at the right time**, improving delivery speed while reducing operational costs.

---

 🎯 Problem Statement

Large retailers often face challenges such as:
- Products stored in the wrong warehouse
- Stock shortages in high-demand locations
- Excess inventory in low-demand locations
- High transportation costs
- Delayed product delivery
- Reduced customer satisfaction

NetworkIQ addresses these challenges using AI-driven recommendations.

---

 💡 Solution

The system analyzes multiple business parameters and recommends:
- Best warehouse for each product
- Stock transfer recommendations
- Quantity to transfer
- Cost-effective inventory placement

---

 📊 AI Input Parameters

The AI makes decisions using:

- Customer Demand
- Current Inventory
- Warehouse Capacity
- Transfer Cost
- Delivery Lead Time
- Product Attributes (Category, Size, Perishability)

## 🔄 Workflow

1. Collect inventory and demand data
2. Analyze demand patterns
3. Check current inventory
4. Verify warehouse capacity
5. Calculate transfer cost
6. Generate AI recommendations
7. Planner approves recommendations
8. Update inventory
